Webb Realty resources

For buyers & sellers

Buying or selling real estate is not as easy as buying a pair of jeans. Educate yourself and work with a qualified, honest agent.

Steps for finding your dream property

  1. Research before you look.
  2. Be realistic about your expectations.
  3. Get your finances in order.
  4. Decide your moving timeline.
  5. Think long term.
  6. Insist on a home inspection.
  7. Get help from a REALTOR®.

Financing

Before you can buy, you need to know how much you can spend. Contact a lender before you start looking so you know what price range to consider.

Webb Realty works with all lenders and values educated, experienced and local professionals.

Transaction stage

Once an offer is accepted, the transaction stage is most often 30–45 days. Your agent opens escrow and helps coordinate inspections, loan deadlines, title work and closing.
